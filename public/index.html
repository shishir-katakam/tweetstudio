Free Tweet to Instagram & X to Instagram converter

TweetStudio is a free tweet to Instagram generator for turning public X posts into polished Instagram-ready posts, carousels and Reels. Paste an X post URL or enter your own text, choose the templates you like, and export square images ready for Instagram.

Turn a tweet into an Instagram carousel

Long posts are automatically paginated into readable square slides instead of being cropped or forced into tiny text. Paragraphs stay together whenever possible, and sentence-aware splitting is used only when necessary.

Generate Instagram graphics from text

You can also generate designs without an X link. Paste any text, optionally add a name, handle, and avatar, and TweetStudio renders the same template collection.

Keep X media in high quality

Attached post media can be previewed at full size, downloaded in its original format, or converted into a square Instagram-friendly version without stretching the source image.

Turn tweets into Reels

Create a 9:16 Reel from an X post: the original account header, highlighted tweet text and the post video are arranged in a clean safe-area layout. If the post has no video, upload your own.

Designed for AI and tech posts

TweetStudio includes editorial, minimalist, high-contrast, and modern social designs built for news, AI announcements, product launches, benchmark results, and technology posts.

How to convert an X post to Instagram

  1. Paste the public X/Twitter post URL, or switch to Text mode.
  2. Click Generate designs to fetch the post data and render the templates.
  3. Preview the carousel slides and any attached media.
  4. Download individual images, the full set, or create a Reel with the Reel Composer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a long tweet to Instagram?

Yes. TweetStudio uses template-aware pagination so long posts can span multiple 1080×1080 slides while preserving the complete text.

Can I generate designs without an X post?

Yes. Use Text mode and paste the content directly. Optional author information can be supplied for branding.

Can I turn an X post into an Instagram Reel?

Yes. The Reel Composer creates a 9:16 layout with the original X account, highlighted tweet text and a video area. A post video loads automatically; if there is no post video, you can upload your own.

Can I download the original media from an X post?

When the public media URL is available, TweetStudio provides an Original download alongside the square Instagram-ready version.

Is TweetStudio an AI image generator?

No. The graphics are rendered with deterministic templates, typography, and layout rules, which keeps generation fast and repeatable.
