TweetStudio

Turn a Tweet into an Instagram Reel

TweetStudio Reel Composer creates a clean 9:16 Reel layout from an X post. The original account name and profile photo appear first, followed by the green divider, tweet text with important words highlighted, and the video.

Use the original post video or your own

If the X post contains a video, TweetStudio loads it automatically. If it does not, upload your own video. Uploading another video replaces the current video instead of overlapping it.

Clean export

The generated Reel contains the actual X post content and video without adding Instagram navigation, like, comment, share or bookmark controls.
